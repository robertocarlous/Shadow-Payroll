# 🌕 Shadow Payroll — Brand Brief

## Tagline

**Private payouts, public proof.**

## Three key messages

1. **Your salary stays yours.** Payroll runs on-chain and fully auditable,
   yet no one — not coworkers, not the public — can see who earns what.
   Each payee's allocation is hidden inside a single Merkle root and claimed
   with a zero-knowledge proof that reveals nothing about identity.
2. **Auditable without revealing.** Anyone can watch the running total and
   confirm the payroll reconciled to the last tNight — the transparency
   blockchains promise, minus the salary leak.
3. **Privacy that compiles into the chain.** Midnight natively runs these
   zero-knowledge proofs in your browser; the guarantee is enforced by the
   smart contract, not by trusting us.

## Color palette

The product runs a light, warm palette — closer to Gusto/Deel/Linear-light
than a dark crypto-trading theme, because Shadow Payroll is a payroll
product first.

- **Warm off-white** — background: `#FAF9F6`
- **Ink** — primary text: `#16151C`
- **Ink soft** — secondary text: `#5C5968`
- **Hairline** — borders: `#E8E5DF`
- **Amber** — the one accent, used sparingly (headline accent, links,
  section numbers, primary CTA is solid ink instead): `#B8791F`
- **Ember rose** — caution/error: `#D1425C`

A dark "terminal" surface (`#14121F`) is used narrowly, for the onboarding
code block and the claim credential textarea — not as a page-level theme.

Full token definitions live in `frontend/src/App.css` (`:root`).

moon-phase glyphs (🌑→🌕) walking across the left third rising to a full
luminous moon on the right, thin violet network-ring lines around it, and the
